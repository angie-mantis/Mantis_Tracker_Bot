⚠️ Never Do These
🚨 Instant Walk-Away Signs
  • Asked for your seed phrase or private key
  • Required to SEND crypto to claim
  • Charges a "gas fee" or "activation fee"
  • Promises guaranteed 100x or 1000x returns
  • Only contact is a random DM — no official site
  • URL looks slightly misspelled (uniswap vs uni5wap)
🛡️ Security Best Practices
🔑
Passwords
Never share your password or PIN with anyone — not support, not admins, not bots. Use a password manager like Bitwarden (free).
🌱
Seed Phrase
Your seed phrase = full wallet access. Write it on paper, store it physically safe. Never type it into any website, app, or bot — ever.
🪣
Burner Wallet
Use a separate "burner" wallet for airdrops — never your main wallet with real funds. If a burner gets drained, losses are minimal.
🔒
2FA
Enable two-factor authentication on every exchange and email. Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) — not SMS.
🛑
Token Approvals
Revoke old token approvals regularly at revoke.cash — unused approvals can be exploited anytime, even years later.
🔗
Links & URLs
Always type URLs yourself or use bookmarks. Scammers buy near-identical misspelled domains and run paid ads for them.
⚙️
Hardware Wallet
Store significant holdings on a hardware wallet (Ledger/Trezor). Even if your computer is hacked, a hardware wallet keeps funds safe.
🎭
Fake Support
Fake support accounts on Telegram and Discord are everywhere. Real admins will NEVER DM you first asking for wallet info.
